## 2. Load-Bearing Triad

The sixth-pass project rests on a load-bearing triad: **biocultural governance** as constitutional ontology (the polity is a complex adaptive system cultivated across nested spatial and temporal scales as a biocultural formation, with biological substrate and cultural plurality coupled, grounded in the biocultural-diversity and biocultural-rights literatures); **eco-mimetic policy-making** as operational method (ecology, complex-adaptive-systems theory, biomimicry, and disturbance-ecology concepts inspire how laws are passed, written, and tested); **disturbance-adapted implementation** as temporal orientation (intergenerational architecture cultivated to absorb a plural disturbance landscape comprising five named regimes — cultural, biological, physical-environmental, cognitive-and-informational, political-economic — and their interactions). The Indigenous and Eastern traditions are the principle's primary articulators; the Western complex-adaptive-systems, ecology, biomimicry, and disturbance-ecology canons are recent and partial recovery. "Grown, not built" is usable as colloquial summary phrase.

For `XII.09`, the triad bears on the foundational decision rights at biosphere-and-ecozone scales: the unidirectional dual currency convertibility (coordinated with `XII.02` currency architecture); the reversible amendment process; and the judicial review of executive orders. Each is specified as a bounded decision right with scope (what the right operates over), scale (the biosphere-and-ecozone scale of the nested-scales reference frame `V.01` articulates), feedback condition (the eco-mimetic-sense feedback architecture `XII.01` operationalizes), and sunset-or-adaptation condition (the temporal architecture under which the right is revised). The sixth-pass revision adds two analytical threads: the sexuality-and-gender-and-reproduction thread, under which bodily and reproductive autonomy are specified as foundational decision rights at biospheric scale; and the defense-and-war-powers thread, which threads through the judicial-review-of-executive-orders right at scope-out-with-threading standing.

### Thread C — Reversible amendment process as foundational decision right at biosphere-and-ecozone scales

The thread specifies the reversible amendment process as a bounded decision right: **scope** — the right operates over the meta-decision of how the constitutional architecture is itself revised; reversibility specifies that no amendment is a final engineered fixture; **scale** — biosphere-and-ecozone, where the meta-decision affects the architecture's capacity to register and respond to biospheric-scale disturbances; **feedback condition** — amendment-and-reversal feedback operates through specified review architecture coordinated with `XII.03`'s e-democracy structural public check; **sunset-or-adaptation condition** — each amendment carries explicit sunset or scheduled-review specification. ### Thread D — Judicial review of executive orders as foundational decision right at biosphere-and-ecozone scales

The thread specifies the judicial review of executive orders as a bounded decision right: **scope** — the right operates over executive-order outputs as decisions reviewable against the foundational normative commitments `I.01` specifies; **scale** — biosphere-and-ecozone, where executive decisions have aggregate-impact reach beyond decision-right scope; **feedback condition** — judicial review is read as feedback architecture in the eco-mimetic sense (cross-referenced to `XII.01`), not as terminal-adjudication architecture; **sunset-or-adaptation condition** — judicial-review outcomes are themselves subject to scheduled review and to the reversible-amendment process Thread C specifies. The thread engages the comparative-constitutional-judicial-review literature through Mark Tushnet's *Weak Courts, Strong Rights* (2008) and the transformative-constitutionalism scholarship through Karl Klare's "Legal Culture and Transformative Constitutionalism" (1998) and Upendra Baxi's *The Future of Human Rights* (2002).

### Thread E — Sexuality, gender, and reproduction: bodily and reproductive autonomy as foundational decision rights (sixth-pass addition)
