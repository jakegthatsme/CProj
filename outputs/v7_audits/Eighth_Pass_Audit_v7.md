# Eighth-Pass Audit — CProj (v7 corpus + eighth-pass increment)

*This audit assesses whether the project's stated goals and objectives have been realized, following the eighth-pass increment that (a) added the standalone war-powers-and-security-state document `XII.13_War_Powers_and_the_Security_State_v7`, (b) threaded it across the corpus and both public-facing derivatives, and (c) advanced the deepening of the non-Western traditions at sites the seventh-pass arc carried at the list level. The audit is deliberately adversarial against the project's own claims, per the instruction to report holes, resolvable tensions, inconsistencies, and fatal flaws where goals are not fully realized. It is written to be read by someone who wants the unvarnished state, not the project's self-presentation.*

## 3. Holes (coverage gaps and unrealized goals)

**H-1 — The non-Western deepening is real but not exhaustive (the principal hole).** The instruction was to *fully* incorporate and deepen all non-Western traditions; the increment advanced this at six documents but left others below parity. Documents whose non-Western (especially Eastern/Islamic/African) engagement remains at or near the list level, or absent, after the increment include at minimum: `VI.03_Energy_as_Cultivation`, `VI.04_Cradle_to_Cradle`, `VI.05_Regenerative_Built_Environment` (which carries traditional architectural canons but thin Eastern/Daoist engagement), `VI.07_Care_Kinship_Social_Reproduction`, `IX.01_Constitutional_Climate_Redesign`, `II.09_Climate_Displacement_and_Belonging`, `XII.07_Harm_Response_Accountability_Repair`, `XII.08_Labor_as_Cultivation`, `XI.01_Three_Operational_Layers`, `IV.01_Deep_Substrate`, `V.01_CAS_Emergence_Adaptive_Governance`, and `VIII.01_Minds_Markets_Sacred` (rich in places, uneven in others). The arc's center of analytical gravity remains Indigenous — which is a defensible *principled* position (the corpus states the Indigenous traditions as the principle's foremost articulators) but is *in tension with* the stated co-equal Indigenous-and-Eastern standing. **Path to closure:** continue the same engagement-depth insertion pattern across the listed documents, prioritizing the material-terrain Part VI (where Eastern agrarian/ecological/medical traditions have the most to add) and the harm-response and labor doors (where Eastern and African restorative and relational-labor traditions are directly relevant). This is bounded, well-specified work; it is simply not yet done. The capstone's Thread F framing — "genuine and realized at depth where the analytical terrain calls for it, but the arc's center of gravity is Indigenous, and the deepening continues" — remains the accurate description, now with more sites deepened than the four it originally named.

**H-2 — "All non-Western traditions" is broader than the increment reached.** Even within the deepened documents, the increment concentrated on the Eastern (Daoist, Buddhist, Confucian), Islamic, and African traditions. Other non-Western and non-dominant traditions the corpus carries — Latin American liberation traditions, Caribbean and Pacific Islander canons, the dalit tradition beyond its existing `II.02`/`VI.02` engagement, Jewish critical traditions — were not separately deepened in this increment (several are already engaged elsewhere in the arc, but the increment did not systematically extend them). **Path to closure:** a tradition-by-tradition coverage matrix (which tradition is engaged at depth in which document) would make the residual gaps precise; it does not yet exist.

**H-3 — `XII.13` bidirectional cross-referencing: CLOSED in this increment.** `XII.13` cites its upstream documents and is now cited back by all the documents whose subject matter it bears on: the capstone `XIV.01`, the decision-rights synthesis `XIII.01`, the bounded-decision-rights document `XII.09`, the political-economy document `X.01`, the capture diagnostic `II.03` (the war-economy / military-industrial capture vector), and the regime-interactions document `II.08` (war as the limiting-case meta-disturbance). The cross-referencing is bidirectional and resolvable; no residue remains for this item. **H-3a — Phase-7δ false-negative class found and FIXED.** Investigating H-3 surfaced a class of collapsed-range artifacts the Phase-7δ repair missed entirely: its detection regex targeted enumerated comma-lists ("Documents II.02, II.02's biological-regime section, …") and specific cognitive-band suffix forms, but not the "through"-range form. A corpus-wide re-run of a same-document range detection found **nine** genuine artifacts across six documents: four backticked `` `XII.09…` through `XII.09…` `` collapses (in `X.01`, `XI.01`, and `XIII.01` ×2 — the original "XII.09 through XII.12" four-document range) and five prose "Documents II.02 through II.02's political-economic-regime section" collapses (in `II.09`, `III.01`, and `IX.01` ×3 — the original "II.02 through II.07" five-regime range). All nine were corrected using the 7δ canonical fix-patterns ("`XII.09…`" / "the consolidated bounded-decision-rights specification … across its four scale bands" with verb agreement repaired; "the five regime sections of Document II.02"), and two stale post-consolidation descriptions ("the four structural-commitments sub-documents/documents") were corrected to the consolidated single-document-with-four-scale-bands form in passing. **H-4 — The operational claims remain untested.** Unchanged by the increment and named throughout the corpus: the architecture's claims (that four-element decision rights resist capture, that the layer separation holds, that the sunset condition cures the ratchet, that cultivation grows rather than freezes) are arguments awaiting practice, not findings from it. ## 4. Resolvable tensions

These are productive analytical tensions the corpus holds open by design. They are not flaws; the cultivation discipline's legitimacy partly consists in holding them rather than resolving them. They are listed so a reader knows they are *chosen* rather than *overlooked*.

**T-1 — Renovation versus decolonial settlement.** The corpus carries the strong decolonial critique (Tuck and Yang, Coulthard, Land Back) at primary-articulator standing while its own architecture occupies the renovation position the critique indicts. Held open at synthesis standing (capstone Thread E). Resolvable only by a standpoint commitment the corpus declines to impose; the honest move is to hold it.

**T-2 — Relational ontology versus the rights-bearer form.** The relational ontologies (Indigenous land-as-relation, Ubuntu, Buddhist interdependence) strain the individual-rights-bearer frame the constitutional form supplies. Held open; partially mediated by routing relational and intergenerational standing into the decision-rights feedback conditions rather than only into individual rights.

**T-3 — Universal provisioning rights versus Indigenous land sovereignty.** A universal right-to-land/food/health can read as diluting the particular sovereignty of specific peoples over specific lands. Mediated by locating provisioning authority, on Indigenous land, in Indigenous sovereignty; the tension is held, not dissolved.

**T-4 — Co-equal Indigenous-and-Eastern standing versus the Indigenous center of gravity (see H-1).** This is the tension the increment most directly engaged and partially relieved. It is resolvable by continued deepening (a quantity-of-work question) rather than by a conceptual choice, which is why it is a hole (H-1) *and* a tension: the gap is closable, and until closed the stated parity overstates the realized parity.

**I-4 — Versioning is mixed.** The increment's new and edited files keep the `_v7` suffix (preserving the position-based naming convention and cross-reference coherence) while their content is described as an "eighth-pass increment." ## 6. Fatal flaws

A fatal flaw would be an internal incoherence or an unmet load-bearing commitment that voids the project's central claim. **The audit finds none of that class.** The central architecture is coherent: the triad, the disturbance diagnostic, the bounded-decision-rights format, the scale-band organization, and the cultivation discipline hang together, and the war-powers increment slots into the existing architecture (meta-disturbance → all five regimes; bounded rights → three bands; sunset condition → the existing four-element format) without strain, which is itself evidence the architecture generalizes. What the audit does record are two **deep vulnerabilities** a serious critic will press, neither of which is fatal but both of which the project must continue to carry honestly rather than claim to have closed:

**V-1 — The appropriation indictment the method cannot fully escape.** The strong decolonial critique holds that no settler-academic synthesis drawing on Indigenous and colonized traditions can be non-extractive, however careful — that primary-articulator standing is still the project choosing whom to carry, and the self-audit is still the extractor grading itself. The project's response (capstone Thread E; Handbook Part Six) is not a refutation but a holding-open: it concedes the renovation position, carries the critique at full strength, and offers honest complicity rather than claimed innocence. Whether this suffices is a standpoint judgment the audit cannot settle. It is the project's deepest vulnerability, and the project's integrity consists precisely in not pretending to have dissolved it. The eighth-pass deepening (H-1) is the most concrete available response — it materially shifts the engagement from naming traditions to working them — but it does not and cannot convert the project into a non-settler one.

**V-2 — Untestedness (H-4 restated as a vulnerability).** The entire architecture is an argument, not a demonstration. Its most important operational claims have never met contact with implementation, and the disturbance-adapted orientation itself implies they would be revised by that contact. A critic is entitled to treat the whole corpus as unfalsified theory. The project's reply — that it offers a cultivation protocol to be grown across generations rather than a finished design to be adopted — reframes the vulnerability but does not remove it.

Neither V-1 nor V-2 is fatal in the sense of internal contradiction; both are the honestly-named outer limits of what a constitutional theory of this kind can claim. The project's chosen posture — hold them open as live disciplines — is, in the audit's judgment, the correct one, and is more defensible than any claimed resolution would be.

## 7. Verdict

The two concrete, well-specified goals of the increment — the war-powers document (G-1) and its threading (G-2) — are **fully realized**, cleanly integrated, and audit-clean (register, cross-references, doubled-word, What-to-Avoid all pass). The open-ended goal — *fully* deepen *all* non-Western traditions (G-3) — is **substantially advanced but not complete**, and the audit declines to overstate it: six documents are materially deepened, roughly a dozen remain below parity (H-1, H-2), and the honest description is the capstone's own — genuine and deepening, with an Indigenous center of gravity and a residual the project carries forward as continued cultivation-discipline work.

The corpus contains **no fatal flaws** of the internal-incoherence class. It contains a bounded set of **resolvable tensions** (T-1–T-4) that are held open by design, a small set of **inconsistencies** (I-1–I-4) that are cosmetic or snapshot-bounded and individually closable with surgical edits, and a set of **holes** (H-1–H-4) that are matters of remaining work rather than of error. Its two **deep vulnerabilities** (V-1 appropriation, V-2 untestedness) are real, are honestly named by the project itself, and are carried as live disciplines rather than concealed.

The shortest path to a still-stronger state, with the cheapest items already executed in this increment: `XII.13` bidirectional cross-referencing is **done** (H-3), and the Phase-7δ collapsed-range false-negative class is **found and fixed** corpus-wide (H-3a). What remains: (1) the Thread F deep-Eastern list and the Thread A document counts, if a non-snapshot capstone is desired (I-1, I-3); (2) continued engagement-depth deepening across the material-terrain and structural-commitment documents named in H-1, ideally guided by the tradition-by-document coverage matrix H-2 calls for; (3) a redundancy-audit re-run over the 36-document corpus. None of these is blocking; all are bounded. The principal genuinely-open item is the breadth of the non-Western deepening (H-1/H-2) — a quantity-of-work matter, honestly partial, not an error.
