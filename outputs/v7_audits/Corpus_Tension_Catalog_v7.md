# Corpus Tension Catalog — CProj v7 (Phase 7β)

*Phase 7β deliverable. Produced by the corpus-wide tension-identification read of the condensed v7 corpus (34 active documents; the 35th, the v7 capstone, is drafted in Phase 7γ). The read prioritized the loci where cross-cutting tensions concentrate: the two consolidated documents (`II.02_Five_Regime_Disturbance_Diagnostic_v7`, `XII.09_Bounded_Decision_Rights_Across_Nested_Scales_v7`, read end-to-end during 7α drafting from their nine v6 source documents), the integrative hubs (the preserved v6 capstone `XIV.01_Capstone_Synthesis_v6` read end-to-end; `XIII.01_Decision_Rights_Synthesis_v7` and `II.08_Disturbance_Regime_Interactions_v7` as the synthesis and interaction hubs; `III.01_Biocultural_Governance_v7`, `XII.01_Eco_Mimetic_Policy_Making_v7`, `V.01`, `VII.01` as the ontological/methodological substrate), and the full cross-reference web that binds the corpus. Items grounded in a full read are marked **[grounded]**; items surfaced through the cross-reference web and integrative reading that warrant confirmation against a full read of a named document in Phase 7δ are marked **[verify]**.*

*Classification follows the planning-document Section XII / `CLAUDE.md` Phase 7β specification: five categories — productive analytical tension; coordination problem; silent gap; thin-versus-rich asymmetry; cross-reference insufficiency. Productive analytical tensions are NOT defects to be repaired; they are the corpus's deliberate analytical commitments, surfaced here so the v7 capstone (Phase 7γ) can read them as such. The other four categories generate the Targeted Revision Targets punch list (`Targeted_Revision_Targets_v7.md`) for Phase 7δ.*

## Category 1 — Productive Analytical Tension

*These tensions are analytically generative and are held deliberately by the corpus. The seventh-pass synthesis reads them as productive; it does not resolve or remove them. The v6 capstone Thread D and the Sixth-Pass planning document Section XII anticipate them by name.*

**1A — Cultivation versus design (the "grown, not built" tension).** [grounded] The corpus's load-bearing move is the substitution of cultivation for design-renovation: the architecture is "grown, not built," specified as a discipline rather than a fixed blueprint (v6 capstone Thread D; `III.01` Thread M; `XIII.01` Thread B). This generates a standing internal tension with the corpus's own *specificity*: the bounded-decision-rights cluster (`XII.09`) specifies fourteen decision rights in a precise four-element format (scope, scale, feedback condition, sunset-or-adaptation condition), and `XII.01` specifies a legislative-cycle and legal-drafting architecture at operational depth. The tension between "specify the architecture precisely" and "refuse engineered-fixture finality" is productive: the four-element format's sunset-or-adaptation condition is itself the corpus's attempt to hold both poles (precise specification + non-finality). The v7 capstone should read this as the corpus's central methodological achievement, not as an inconsistency.

**1B — Renovation versus decolonial (settled-but-live).** [grounded] The v6 capstone Thread D gives the project's "settled" position: cultivation is legitimate under the multi-traditional canonical commitment and is not synthesis-as-appropriation, operationalized through three disciplines (cultivation-as-discipline-not-architecture; cross-citation-not-synthesis-into-single-vocabulary; ongoing-not-fixed-at-founding). The settlement is explicitly *not* foreclosure: the decolonial critique (Mignolo, Quijano, Lugones, de Sousa Santos, Tuck and Yang, Alcoff) remains a live methodological discipline within the project. This is the corpus's deepest productive tension — a constitutional synthesis that holds open the critique of constitutional synthesis. The v7 capstone inherits this at synthesis standing and must perform it (not merely cite it) across the consolidated corpus.

**1C — Disturbance-regime framing versus structural-commitment framing.** [grounded] Part II diagnoses the polity through five disturbance regimes (now consolidated in `II.02`); Part XII specifies the positive architecture through bounded decision rights across four scale bands (now consolidated in `XII.09`). The two framings are organized on *different axes* — `II.02` by regime (cultural/biological/physical-environmental/cognitive/political-economic), `XII.09` by scale band (biosphere-ecozone/ecosystem-habitat/habitat-population/organism-and-temporal). `II.08` and the dependent-origination engagement (II.08 Thread C, via Nāgārjuna and Joanna Macy) hold that the regime distinctions are "analytical conveniences the cross-regime interactions continuously dissolve." This is productive: the diagnostic and prescriptive halves of the corpus are deliberately incommensurable in their organizing axis, and the integrative work (II.08; XIII.01; the v7 capstone) is precisely the bridge across them. (See also Silent Gap 3C — the *explicit* regime-to-scale-band mapping is not drawn anywhere; that part is a gap, the framing-incommensurability itself is productive.)

**1D — Multi-traditional canon: tensions across co-equal primary articulators.** [grounded] The corpus engages dozens of traditions at co-equal canonical standing (v6 capstone Thread E enumerates Indigenous, Eastern, African, Andean, Maori, Pacific Islander, Black-radical, Caribbean, dalit, Latin American, disability-justice, neurodivergent, feminist, Latinx, Asian-American, climate-justice, Islamic, Liberation-Theology, Russian/Eastern-European, and Jewish canons). Several make claims in genuine tension with one another — the dalit anti-caste tradition (Ambedkar) against inheritances that some Eastern traditions carry; the Black-radical racial-capitalism frame (Cedric Robinson) against the Indigenous land-sovereignty-primacy frame (Coulthard, the Land Back articulation), a tension Tiffany Lethabo King's *The Black Shoals* explicitly works at the Black-Native seam; the universalist register of some rights-traditions against the relational-ontology register of others. The corpus's commitment is to hold these in their own analytical registers (cross-citation, not synthesis), so the tensions are preserved rather than dissolved. Productive — but the v7 capstone should name the specific cross-tradition seams rather than gesturing at "the multi-traditional canon" as a unified block (see Thin-vs-Rich 4B).

## Category 2 — Coordination Problem

*A doctrine or structure specified one way in one document that operates in tension with its specification elsewhere, requiring targeted alignment (Phase 7δ).*

**2A — The consolidation's structural change is not yet reflected in the corpus's integrative self-description.** [grounded] The defining coordination problem the v7 condensation introduces. The corpus now contains two consolidated documents (`II.02` = five regimes in one document; `XII.09` = four scale bands in one document), but the integrative and interaction documents still describe the corpus *as if the regime diagnostics and the D-series were separate documents*. `II.08_Disturbance_Regime_Interactions_v7` is the clearest case: its analytical prose enumerates "the five disturbance-regime diagnostics of Part II (cultural in Document II.02, biological in Document II.02's biological-regime section, physical-environmental in Document II.02's physical-environmental-regime section, …)" and refers to "Documents XII.09 through XII.09's cognitive-and-intergenerational scale band." These references are now *resolvable* (the 7α repair eliminated all orphans), but the prose reads as if five/four separate documents still exist. This is a coordination problem between the consolidated structure and the corpus's description of it. It surfaces wherever a document enumerated the regime diagnostics or the D-series as a set or a range. → Punch-list items P-1 (II.08), P-2 (XIII.01 verify), P-3 (corpus-wide sweep).

**2B — The four-element format's phase vocabulary is coordinated (verified — NOT a problem).** [grounded] A candidate coordination problem checked and cleared: `XII.09`'s sunset-or-adaptation conditions reference the panarchy adaptive-cycle phases ("r-phase … K-phase … Ω-phase … α-phase," "conservation-phase," "release-phase," "reorganization"), and `XII.01_Eco_Mimetic_Policy_Making_v7` Thread E uses the identical vocabulary. The two are aligned. Recorded here so the v7 capstone and Phase 7δ do not re-open it. No action.

**2C — Reproductive autonomy's four-register specification is coordinated (verified — NOT a problem).** [grounded] The sexuality-and-gender-and-reproduction thread is specified at four registers within the now-single `XII.09` (foundational / public-check / provisioning / organism-scale) and at the embodiment axis of the now-single `II.02`. Because both are consolidated into single documents, the cross-register coordination that previously spanned five separate documents (XII.09–XII.12 plus II.02) is now intra-document and internally consistent. The 7α consolidation *improved* this coordination. No action; note as a consolidation benefit for the v7 capstone to register.

## Category 3 — Silent Gap

*A commitment or coupling that becomes visible as missing only when the whole corpus is in hand.*

**3A — No document yet reads the corpus at its consolidated (35-document) structure.** [grounded] Every integrative document in the active corpus (`XIII.01`, `II.08`) was authored against the 42-document structure and carried forward through cross-reference repair only. The v6 capstone (preserved in place) reads the 42-document arc. Until the v7 capstone (Phase 7γ), *no document performs the arc-wide synthesis at the consolidated structure* — the structural fact that Part II's diagnostics and Part XII's decision rights are each now a single document with internal sections/bands, and what that consolidation reveals analytically (e.g., that the five-regime diagnostic and the four-scale-band architecture are each more legible as a single coordinated specification). Closing this gap is the defining work of Phase 7γ; the catalog records it so the v7 capstone plan treats it as a primary commitment, not an incidental update.

**3B — The defense-and-war-powers standalone treatment (deliberately deferred).** [grounded] The v6 capstone Thread G explicitly flags the scope-out-with-threading of defense and war powers as "a defensible sixth-pass scope decision that a future pass might revisit — a Part-level standalone document on defense, war powers, and the constitutional commitments to non-militarism would be a substantive addition the seventh-pass synthesis or the broader project could take up." The v7 condensation (moderate scope, two clusters) does *not* add it; the thread remains scope-out-with-threading at `XII.09`'s Scale Bands One and Two. This is a known, deliberately-deferred gap. The catalog records it; the v7 capstone should re-flag it as eighth-pass-or-later candidate work (per the planning document Section XII, Deferred Work), not silently drop the v6 capstone's flag.

**3C — The regime-to-scale-band mapping is implicit, never drawn.** [grounded] `II.02` organizes the diagnostic by five regimes; `XII.09` organizes the prescription by four scale bands. No document draws the explicit mapping between *which regimes are absorbed by which scale-band decision rights*. The couplings exist implicitly (e.g., `II.02`'s political-economic regime ↔ `XII.09` Scale Band One's currency convertibility; `II.02`'s cognitive regime ↔ `XII.09` Scale Band Two's technological-critique response and Scale Band Four's neurodivergent-polis conditions), and `II.08` carries the cross-regime feedback conditions "the rest of the arc downstream of Part II depends on," but the diagnostic-to-prescription bridge at the consolidated level is not explicitly tabulated. This is a silent gap the v7 capstone is well-positioned to close (it is exactly the corpus-wide integrative move the per-document work cannot carry). Not a Phase 7δ surgical-edit target — it is capstone (7γ) work.

## Category 4 — Thin-versus-Rich Asymmetry

*An element articulated thinly in one place that requires strengthening when read against its richer articulation elsewhere — candidate for a surgical Phase 7δ strengthening (a paragraph or two), never a full rewrite.*

**4A — The defense-and-war-powers thread is thin relative to the other four sixth-pass threads.** [grounded] By the v6 capstone Thread G's own account, the defense-and-war-powers thread runs through only two carrier registers (now `XII.09` Scale Bands One and Two) at scope-out-with-threading standing, whereas the sexuality-and-gender-and-reproduction, communications-and-media-and-press, technology-and-AI, and arts-and-language-and-cultural-production threads each run through three or more carriers with substantially richer multi-traditional primary-articulator engagement. This asymmetry is *partly deliberate* (the scope-out is principled). It is recorded as thin-vs-rich rather than silent-gap because the thinness is a known scope decision, not an oversight; the v7 capstone's comparison-gesture work should register the asymmetry explicitly rather than presenting the five threads as evenly developed.

**4B — Eastern primary articulators: stated co-equal standing versus realized analytical depth.** [verify] The corpus names the Indigenous *and Eastern* traditions as co-equal primary articulators of all three triad elements (v6 capstone Threads A, E; `III.01`; `XII.01`). The realized engagement is asymmetric: the Eastern traditions (Daoist *wu wei*/*ziran*, Buddhist dependent-origination/Abhidhamma, Confucian *li*/remonstrance, Islamic *shura*/*waqf*) are engaged at genuine analytical depth in a handful of documents — `II.02`'s cognitive-informational regime section (Buddhist/Daoist consciousness analysis), `III.01` Thread E (Nāgārjuna), `II.08` Thread C (Macy's dependent-origination/systems bridge), `XII.01` (the Eastern method articulators) — but appear in many other documents as named-articulator *lists* rather than as engaged analytical apparatus, where the Indigenous traditions carry the substantive analytical load throughout. This is a thin-vs-rich asymmetry between the stated co-equal standing and the realized depth. The v7 capstone (Thread E equivalent) should either name the asymmetry honestly or the corpus should strengthen specific thin Eastern-engagement passages. Flagged **[verify]** because confirming the breadth requires a full read of the documents not yet read end-to-end (Part VI, Part X, Part XI, and the I-series).

**4C — `IV.01_Deep_Substrate` is described thinly in the integrative documents.** [verify] The v6 capstone Thread B treats Part IV (the meta-rights and pre-rights commitments — "the right to exist, the right to grow, the right to be in relation") almost entirely by cross-citation, and the document is one of the least cross-referenced in the corpus relative to its claimed foundational ("deep substrate") standing. This may be appropriate (a genuinely foundational document need not be heavily downstream-cited) or may be a thin-vs-rich asymmetry where a load-bearing foundation is under-integrated. Flagged **[verify]** for a full read of `IV.01` and a scan of its downstream uptake before any 7δ action.

## Category 5 — Cross-Reference Insufficiency

*References that resolve but are imprecise, stale, or grammatically broken — the surgical core of Phase 7δ.*

**5A — Consolidation-repair prose artifacts in enumerating documents.** [grounded] The 7α cross-reference repair correctly made every reference resolvable (orphan `_v6` count 0; freed-ordinal cross-references in rebadged docs 0), but where a document *enumerated* the regime diagnostics or the D-series as a set or a range, the mechanical per-ordinal substitution produced clunky or grammatically broken prose. Confirmed instances in `II.08_Disturbance_Regime_Interactions_v7`: the repetitive "Documents II.02, II.02's biological-regime section, II.02's physical-environmental-regime section, II.02's cognitive-and-informational-regime section, and II.02's political-economic-regime section" (appears multiple times), and the broken range "Documents XII.09 through XII.09's cognitive-and-intergenerational scale band." These should be revised to clean appositive prose ("the five regime sections of Document II.02"; "the four scale bands of Document XII.09"). → Punch-list P-1.

**5B — Other enumerating/range references across the corpus.** [verify] The same repair pattern will have produced analogous artifacts wherever any of the other 31 documents enumerated the five regime diagnostics or the four D-series documents as a set or range. `XIII.01_Decision_Rights_Synthesis_v7` (the synthesis hub, which by design integrates "the four D-series documents" and "the eleven elements") is the highest-probability site and must be read in full in Phase 7δ. A corpus-wide grep for the repaired-artifact signatures ("II.02's …-regime section, II.02's", "XII.09 through XII.09's", "XII.09's … scale band, XII.09's") is the mechanical detection step. → Punch-list P-2, P-3.

**5C — Stale provenance-label metadata in rebadged documents.** [grounded] Each rebadged document's header note retains a label such as "Sixth-pass position-based name: `<name>_v7`" and v6-venue output-path metadata (e.g., "`project/source_documents/v6_completed/…_v7.md`"), now carrying a `_v7` suffix on a "Sixth-pass" label and a `v6_completed` path. These are historical-metadata lines, not active cross-references; they resolve to nothing programmatic and do not orphan (no `_v6` token remains). They are cosmetic staleness. Low priority; batchable in a single 7δ metadata pass if undertaken at all. → Punch-list P-4 (optional).

**5D — The v6 capstone's 42-document references (no action — preserved by design).** [grounded] `XIV.01_Capstone_Synthesis_v6` (preserved in place at `v6_completed/`, replaced not superseded) references the full 42-document structure throughout. This is correct and must not be repaired: the v6 capstone is a legitimate artifact of the pre-consolidation, pre-synthesis state. The v7 capstone (Phase 7γ) is the document that reads the consolidated structure. Recorded so no future repair pass touches the v6 capstone.
