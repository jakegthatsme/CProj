## PART ONE — COGNITIVE-PLURALITY COMMITMENT AS CULTIVATION CONDITION AT ORGANISM-AND-ORGAN-SYSTEM SCALE

### 1.1 The commitment articulated

The cognitive-plurality commitment specifies that the polity's cognitive substrate at organism-and-organ-system scale is cultivated as plural rather than normalized. The substrate operates at the level of the individual cognitive organism — the human reasoner whose neurobiological and cognitive-functional architecture exhibits substantial population-level variation across multiple dimensions — and at the level of the cognitive-functional organ-system architecture within the organism, which integrates perceptual, attentional, memory, executive-function, social-cognitive, and affective subsystems whose specific configurations vary across the population. The cultivation commitment is that constitutional architecture supports this variation rather than suppressing it through the normalization apparatus the contemporary configuration has institutionalized.

The argument operates against two background positions. The first is the medical-model position that frames cognitive variation outside a statistical norm as deficit requiring intervention oriented to normalization. The neurodiversity movement and the broader disability-justice and Mad-studies traditions have articulated the structural-political case against the medical-model position at length, with the substantive claim that the medical-model framing systematically pathologizes variation that operates within the broader range of human cognitive functioning. The second is the social-model position that operates as critical alternative to the medical model but that has been criticized within the disability-justice tradition for inadequate engagement with the substantive bodymind content of disability experience — for treating disability as fundamentally constructed by social arrangements without adequately engaging the bodymind realities the disability-justice tradition centers. The cognitive-plurality commitment operates within neither the medical-model nor the strict-social-model framing but engages the substantive bodymind content of cognitive variation as one dimension of the broader cognitive-plurality the cultivation work supports.

### 1.2 The hunter-gatherer cognitive baseline and the bounded-rational-actor critique

The cultivation commitment coordinates with `IV.01_Deep_Substrate_v7`'s hunter-gatherer cognitive baseline as evolved cognitive substrate. The human cognitive architecture evolved across approximately three hundred thousand years of *Homo sapiens* evolution under conditions of hunter-gatherer social organization, with the specific cognitive-architectural features — the perceptual systems, the attentional architecture, the social-cognitive apparatus, the affective-regulatory systems — adapted to the cultivation conditions of hunter-gatherer life. The contemporary configuration places these cognitive-architectural features under conditions of substantial mismatch with the evolved baseline: the contemporary informational landscape, the contemporary work configuration, the contemporary social-organization configuration, the contemporary educational apparatus, the broader contemporary configuration all operate under conditions distant from the evolved baseline.

The implication for the cognitive-plurality commitment is that the contemporary configuration's normalization apparatus operates against the broader population-level variation the hunter-gatherer cognitive baseline produced. The configuration's selection pressures favor a narrow band of cognitive functioning adapted to specific contemporary tasks (sustained attention in office configurations, specific kinds of executive-function, specific kinds of social-cognitive processing aligned with contemporary norms) and pathologize the variation that lies outside this narrow band. The cognitive-plurality commitment operates against the normalization apparatus and supports the broader population-level variation as cultivation condition.

The bounded-rational-actor critique `II.01_Biological_Evolutionary_Anthropological_Critique_v7` carries supplies parallel analytical foundation. The inherited political-economic apparatus operates on the rational-actor model that systematically misrepresents the empirical cognitive functioning of human reasoners, with the cognitive-plurality commitment specifying that the constitutional architecture engages the empirical bounded-rational-actor reality across its population-level variation rather than presupposing a uniform rational-actor cognitive baseline.

### 1.3 The cultivation condition at organism-and-organ-system scale

The cultivation condition operates at organism-and-organ-system scale of the nested-scales reference frame. The scale specifies the cultivation work at the level of the individual cognitive organism (the human reasoner as cognitive organism with specific neurobiological and cognitive-functional architecture) and at the level of the cognitive-functional organ-system architecture within the organism. The cultivation work at this scale includes: the cultivation of neurodivergent-affirming educational architecture (developed at Part Seven and at `XII.06_Pedagogy_as_Constitutional_Cultivation_v7`); the cultivation of neurodivergent-affirming employment architecture; the cultivation of neurodivergent-affirming healthcare architecture; the cultivation of neurodivergent-affirming civic-participation architecture (developed at Part Seven and at `XII.03_E_Democracy_Structural_Public_Check_v7`); the cultivation of accommodations architecture across the broader institutional configuration; and the broader content of the cultivation work the contemporary disability-justice, Mad-studies, and neurodivergent-constitutional-articulator traditions specify.
