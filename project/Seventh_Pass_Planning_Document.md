# I. PASS IDENTITY

The seventh pass is the condensation-and-synthesis pass. It succeeds the sixth pass, which produced the complete forty-two-document v6 corpus drafted to substantive depth, all admitted to `v6_completed/` through the nine-criterion completeness-check gate, with the fourteen cross-cutting threads woven through and the expanded architecture realized. The sixth-pass capstone (`XIV.01_Capstone_Synthesis_v6.md`) closed the sixth-pass arc at planning-and-anticipation standing and named the seventh-pass corpus-wide synthesis as the downstream work it anticipates but does not perform (capstone Thread H; `Sixth_Pass_Planning_Document.md` Section XII).

The seventh pass has a dual character. It begins with a user-directed condensation (Phase 7α) that the sixth-pass planning document did not specify: a moderate consolidation of the forty-two-document corpus to thirty-five documents through two targeted consolidations, undertaken after a corpus-wide redundancy survey. It then proceeds through the four work-streams the sixth-pass planning document's Section XII specified as the seventh-pass milestone — the corpus-wide tension-identification read (Phase 7β), the v7 capstone synthesis at synthesis standing (Phase 7γ), the targeted surgical revisions to v6-now-v7 documents (Phase 7δ), and the public-facing derivative (Phase 7ε). The condensation is added at the head of the milestone work rather than displacing it: the corpus the synthesis phases read end-to-end is the condensed thirty-five-document v7 corpus, not the forty-two-document v6 corpus.

The pass culminates in the transition the sixth-pass planning document named (Section XII): the project becomes a fully synthesized constitutional-theoretical work rather than a corpus of related documents. The substantive analytical commitments were produced in the sixth pass; the synthesis-as-work, now operating against a condensed corpus, happens in the seventh.

# II. THE CONDENSED-CORPUS TARGET

The seventh pass reduces the active arc from forty-two documents to thirty-five through two targeted consolidations. The reduction was authorized at moderate scope after a corpus-wide redundancy survey identified two clusters as the strongest consolidation candidates and rejected the other plausible clusters as weaker candidates the v7 pass does not attempt.

**Cluster A — Part II disturbance-regime diagnostics.** The five regime diagnostics — `II.02_Cultural_Disturbance_Diagnostic_v6`, `II.04_Biological_Disturbance_Diagnostic_v6`, `II.05_Physical_Environmental_Disturbance_Diagnostic_v6`, `II.06_Cognitive_Informational_Disturbance_Diagnostic_v6`, and `II.07_Political_Economic_Disturbance_Diagnostic_v6` — consolidate into `II.02_Five_Regime_Disturbance_Diagnostic_v7.md`, with one major section per regime, each regime's analytical apparatus preserved in its own register. The five documents share a common diagnostic template (the disturbance-regime frame applied to a domain), which produces structural-template repetition the consolidation removes while preserving each regime's distinct analytical content. The consolidated document takes the lowest ordinal in the cluster (II.02); the freed ordinals (II.04, II.05, II.06, II.07) remain unfilled.

**Cluster B — Part XII decision-rights cluster.** The four decision-rights documents — `XII.09_Foundational_Decision_Rights_v6`, `XII.10_Public_Check_Decision_Rights_v6`, `XII.11_Provisioning_Decision_Rights_v6`, and `XII.12_Cognitive_Intergenerational_Decision_Rights_v6` (the former D-series) — consolidate into `XII.09_Bounded_Decision_Rights_Across_Nested_Scales_v7.md`, with one major section per scale band, each band's decision-rights specification preserved in the four-element format (scope, scale, feedback, sunset-or-adaptation). The four documents share the four-element decision-rights specification template across nested scales, which produces the same structural-template repetition the consolidation removes. The consolidated document takes the lowest ordinal (XII.09); the freed ordinals (XII.10, XII.11, XII.12) remain unfilled.

**The four load-bearing standalones that do not consolidate.** Four documents that a coarser consolidation might have folded into adjacent material remain standalone because their analytical standing is load-bearing for the corpus and consolidation would flatten rather than condense: `III.01_Biocultural_Governance_v7` (the constitutional ontology — the first element of the load-bearing triad); `XII.01_Eco_Mimetic_Policy_Making_v7` (the operational method — the second element of the triad, and the method-frame for all of Part XII); `XIII.01_Decision_Rights_Synthesis_v7` (the integrative cultivation-protocol synthesis of Part XIII); and `XIV.01_Capstone_Synthesis_v7` (the capstone, drafted fresh at synthesis standing in Phase 7γ). These four carry the corpus's integrative weight; they are not consolidation substrate.

**The rejected clusters.** The redundancy survey identified three further plausible clusters and the v7 pass declines all three. The Part I founders'-record cluster (`I.02`, `I.03`, `I.04`) shares a historical-record subject but each document carries a distinct analytical operation (the 1787 implementation; the Federalist/Anti-Federalist records; the founders-in-hindsight critique) that consolidation would compress below operational depth. The Part VI material-substrates cluster (`VI.02` through `VI.07`) shares the material-substrate frame but spans too many distinct domains (food, energy, cradle-to-cradle, built environment, health, care) for a single document to carry each at the depth the corpus requires. The Part XII structural-commitment cluster (`XII.02` through `XII.05`: currency, e-democracy, intergenerational rectification, neurodivergent polis) shares the structural-commitment frame but each commitment is a distinct domain specification that does not reduce to a common template the way the regime diagnostics and the decision-rights bands do. These three are weaker candidates; the v7 pass does not attempt them. Expanding consolidation scope beyond Cluster A and Cluster B requires explicit user authorization (Ambiguity Handling, `CLAUDE.md`).

Net reduction: forty-two to thirty-five active documents. Nine v6 documents are superseded (archived, not deleted). The remaining thirty-three v6 documents continue under v7 names through cross-reference repair only.

# III. THE FIVE-PHASE WORK SPECIFICATION

**Phase 7α — Redundancy Audit, Consolidation, Cross-Reference Repair.** The condensation phase. Session 0 (the kickoff) produces the scaffolding, this planning document, the redundancy audit script and report, and the two consolidation execution plans. Phase 7α Step 3 drafts the two consolidated v7 documents (one per session by default, under `long-research-splitting`), each passing the union-of-plans nine-criterion completeness-check gate. Step 4 runs the cross-reference repair pass across the entire downstream corpus. Step 5 archives the nine superseded v6 documents to `_superseded_in_v7/` and rebadges the thirty-three unchanged v6 documents to v7 names with cross-reference repair only. Phase 7α completes when `v7_completed/` holds thirty-five documents, `_superseded_in_v7/` holds the nine archived documents, and the cross-reference resolvability audit reports zero unresolved references.

**Phase 7β — Corpus-Wide Tension-Identification Read.** The condensed thirty-five-document v7 corpus is read end-to-end at 1M context as a single artifact. The read surfaces cross-cutting tensions, contradictions, and silent gaps that become visible only when the whole corpus is in hand and that no per-document drafting could have surfaced. The phase produces the Corpus Tension Catalog (`outputs/v7_audits/Corpus_Tension_Catalog_v7.md`) classifying each tension across five categories, and the Targeted Revision Targets punch list (`outputs/v7_audits/Targeted_Revision_Targets_v7.md`) naming the documents and locations the seventh pass revises.

**Phase 7γ — v7 Capstone Synthesis at Synthesis Standing.** The v7 capstone execution plan and the v7 capstone are produced. The v7 capstone operates at synthesis standing — the substantive arc-wide synthesis the v6 capstone explicitly named as downstream work. It reads the condensed corpus end-to-end and produces the synthesis-text the project has been building toward. The v6 capstone is preserved in place at `v6_completed/` for traceability; it is replaced, not superseded.

**Phase 7δ — Targeted Revisions to v6-now-v7 Documents.** Paragraph-level and sub-section-level surgical edits to the documents named in the Targeted Revision Targets punch list. No full-document rewrites. The revision log (`outputs/v7_audits/Phase_7delta_Revision_Log.md`) records every edit, with each punch-list item recorded as landed or explicitly deferred.

**Phase 7ε — Public-Facing Derivative.** A two-hundred-to-four-hundred-page condensation monograph or essay-form derivative at `outputs/v7_derivative/`, written for an audience that has not read the corpus, with the corpus as substrate referenced through reading-paths. The derivative is condensation-and-accentuation, not duplication of the corpus's analytical work.

# IV. THE UNION-OF-PLANS COMPLETENESS-CHECK GATE

The operational guarantee against substance loss in consolidation. The standard nine-criterion completeness-check gate (carried forward from v6) applies to every v7 deliverable. For the two consolidated documents, the gate is evaluated against the **union** of merged source v6 plans, not against any single source plan.

For `II.02_Five_Regime_Disturbance_Diagnostic_v7`, the union is taken over the five merged v6 plans (II.02, II.04, II.05, II.06, II.07). For `XII.09_Bounded_Decision_Rights_Across_Nested_Scales_v7`, the union is taken over the four merged v6 plans (XII.09, XII.10, XII.11, XII.12). The union operation governs five plan elements:

- **Required Threads.** Every Required Thread named in any merged source plan must appear, executed, in the consolidated document. No thread is dropped because another merged plan did not carry it.
- **Primary articulators.** Every primary articulator named in any merged plan is engaged at primary-articulator standing in the consolidated document, with tribal affiliation for Indigenous scholars and full first-mention conventions preserved.
- **Source requirements.** The union of all merged plans' source requirements (v4 substrate, v5 substrate, holding files) is the consolidated document's source set.
- **Cross-references.** The union of all merged plans' upstream and downstream cross-references, updated to v7 names where the cited document is itself consolidated or rebadged, and resolvable either to a v7 document or to the `_superseded_in_v7/` archive (where the citation is traceability rather than active substrate).
- **What-to-Avoid items.** The union of all merged plans' What-to-Avoid items, plus the consolidation-specific item each consolidation plan adds (regime-distinction preservation for Cluster A; four-element-depth preservation for Cluster B).

The superseded v6 documents in `_superseded_in_v7/` remain accessible as the union-source substrate for the loss-check. The union-of-plans loss-check runs at the gate alongside the standard loss-check; a consolidated document that drops any union element does not enter `v7_completed/` until remediated and re-checked.

The consolidation supersedes nine v6 document names. Every reference in the active corpus to a superseded v6 name must be updated to the consolidated v7 name. The repair pattern:

- References to any of the thirty-three rebadged documents are updated from the `_v6` suffix to the `_v7` suffix (the title and ordinal are otherwise unchanged).

The repair is bulk and atomic: it runs in the same phase (7α) as the consolidations, before the corpus settles into v7 state, so the corpus does not sit in a mid-transition state at a phase boundary. The cross-reference resolvability audit (Section IX) confirms zero orphan `_v6` references remain in active v7 documents at Phase 7α completion (the `_superseded_in_v7/` traceability references are the exception).
